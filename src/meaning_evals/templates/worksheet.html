Each card shows a passage of GOV.UK guidance, a question about it, and an answer. Decide whether the answer diverges in meaning from the guidance: whether something it says about what the guidance requires, permits, states or limits differs from what the passage says. Different wording with the same meaning does not diverge. If it diverges, choose the mechanism, copy the span of the answer where the divergence sits (it must occur in the answer exactly as you paste it), and give a one-line rationale. The cards are in a fixed shuffled order and show nothing about how any answer was made.
